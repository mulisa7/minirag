Coffee beans are the seeds of the coffee plant. Arabica makes up most of the world's production and has a sweeter, more complex taste. Robusta has roughly twice the caffeine and a harsher, more bitter flavour.

Espresso is made by forcing hot water through finely ground coffee at high pressure. A typical shot takes 25 to 30 seconds to pull. Brewing with water that is too hot makes the coffee bitter.
