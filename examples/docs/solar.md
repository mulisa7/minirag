# The Solar System

The Sun contains about 99.86 percent of the mass in the solar system. Jupiter is the largest planet. It is more than twice as massive as all the other planets combined.

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and completes an orbit in 88 days. Venus is the hottest planet because its thick atmosphere traps heat, even though Mercury is nearer the Sun.

Mars has the tallest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons, which is about three times the height of Mount Everest.
